zote /zōt/

noun

A collectible that lives on Zcash as a shielded note, in a vault only your key controls. You prove you own it by answering a challenge, not by pointing at someone's records.

It's under your key

Most Zcash collectibles today are entries a marketplace keeps. A zote is a note on the chain that only your key can spend. No company holds the list.

Anyone you show it to can check it

Hand someone a proof and they can confirm your zote is genuine, traces back to the sealed collection, and is still yours. Fakes and old owners fail.

Nobody can watch it

On the chain, a sale looks like any other shielded transaction. Who owns what, who bought and who sold stay private.
